Warm up by the fire, free for everyone

Musher's Rest is our open-air cabin with a cozy fire burning inside, and it's free to step into any time during your visit. Whether you're waiting between activities or just need to thaw out your fingers, this is the spot: seating is available, and everyone is welcome to relax, chat, and enjoy the warmth for as long as they like.


Good to know


  • No booking needed, just walk in whenever you like
  • Seating is available, but can fill up on busy days
  • It's a great spot to snap some photos by the fire 📸
